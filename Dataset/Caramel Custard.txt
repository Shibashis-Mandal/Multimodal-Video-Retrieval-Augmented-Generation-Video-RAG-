Caramel Custard


Ingredients


For the caramel


* ½ cup (100 g) sugar
* 1 tbsp water
* A pinch of pink salt


For the custard


* 350 ml milk
* 2 whole eggs
* 1 egg yolk
* 2 tbsp sugar
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* A pinch of pink salt


Method


Step 1: Make the caramel


1. Add the sugar, water, and a pinch of pink salt to a saucepan.
2. Cook over medium heat without stirring until the sugar melts and turns a deep amber colour.
3. Immediately pour the caramel into your mould(s), swirling to coat the base evenly.
4. Let it cool completely until the caramel becomes hard.


Step 2: Prepare the custard


1. Heat the milk in a saucepan.
2. Add the sugar and stir until it dissolves completely.
3. Do not let the milk boil. As soon as light steam begins to rise, remove it from the heat.
4. In a separate bowl, beat the 2 whole eggs and 1 egg yolk until just combined.
5. Slowly pour the hot milk into the eggs while whisking continuously to prevent the eggs from scrambling.
6. Stir in the vanilla extract and a pinch of pink salt.


Step 3: Fill the mould


1. Once the caramel has completely set and hardened, pour the custard mixture into the mould.
2. If desired, strain the mixture through a fine sieve for an extra-smooth texture.


Step 4: Steam


1. Place the mould in a steamer.
2. Steam for 30–40 minutes, depending on the size of the mould.
3. Check for doneness by inserting a toothpick into the centre. If it comes out clean, the custard is ready.


Step 5: Chill and demould


1. Allow the custard to cool to room temperature.
2. Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled (at least 4–6 hours, preferably overnight).
3. Run a knife around the edge of the mould, place a serving plate on top, and invert to release the caramel custard.
