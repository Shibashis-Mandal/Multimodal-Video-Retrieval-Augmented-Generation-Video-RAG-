Yogurt Chicken
Ingredients
For the Marinade
* 1 kg chicken
* 250 g plain sour yogurt
* 1½ tsp salt (or to taste)
* 1 tbsp lemon juice
* 1½ tbsp ginger
* 1½ tbsp garlic
* 4–5 green chilies
* 1½ tsp coriander powder
* ½ tsp garam masala powder
Tip: If you're cooking for more than 3–4 people, blend 1 medium onion into the marinade. It will give you a little extra gravy.
For Cooking
* 3 large onions, thinly sliced
* 4–5 tbsp mustard oil or any neutral cooking oil
* 1 tbsp ghee
* 4 green cardamoms
* 4–5 cloves
* 1-inch cinnamon stick
* 8–10 whole black peppercorns
* 10–12 cashew nuts
* ½ tsp black pepper powder
* 2–3 green chilies, slit
⸻
Method
1. In a bowl, combine the yogurt with salt, lemon juice, ginger, garlic, green chili paste, coriander powder, and garam masala. Coat the chicken thoroughly with this marinade. Let it marinate for at least 1½ hours, though overnight is ideal for the best flavor.
2. Heat the oil in a pan and slowly fry the sliced onions until they turn a light golden brown (beresta). Avoid browning them too much.
3. In the same oil, add the ghee, followed by the cardamoms, cloves, cinnamon, and whole black peppercorns. Let the whole spices release their aroma.
4. Add the marinated chicken and stir over medium heat for about 2 minutes. Then cover the pan with a lid.
5. After 5 minutes, uncover, give it a good stir, and cover again. The chicken will release enough moisture as it cooks, so there is absolutely no need to add even a single drop of water.
6. Continue cooking this way, stirring occasionally and covering again, until the chicken is about 80% cooked through.
7. Blend most of the fried beresta with the cashew nuts and a little water into a smooth paste.
8. Stir this paste into the chicken. From this point onward, do not cover the pan. Since the cashews can cause the gravy to stick to the bottom, keep stirring continuously while cooking until the gravy reaches your preferred consistency. Keep in mind that the gravy will thicken a little more after removing it from the heat.
9. Finish with the black pepper powder, slit green chilies, and the remaining beresta sprinkled on top. Remove from the heat.
Serve hot with steamed rice, pulao, or parathas.
