Coconut Rice


Ingredients:


* 1 cup basmati rice
* 1 cup thick coconut milk
* 1 cup water
* 1 tbsp coconut oil
* 1 bay leaf
* 1 cinnamon stick
* 2 green cardamoms
* 3 cloves
* ¾ tsp salt (or to taste)
* ½ tsp sugar


⸻


Method


1. Wash the rice well and soak it for 20 minutes. Drain completely.
2. Heat coconut oil in a heavy-bottomed pot. Add the bay leaf, cinnamon stick, cardamoms and cloves. Sauté for 20–30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add the drained rice and gently sauté for 1 minute so every grain gets coated with the oil.
4. Pour in the coconut milk and water. Add the salt and sugar, then mix gently.
5. Bring it to a gentle boil, cover with a tight-fitting lid and cook on the lowest heat for 12–15 minutes.
6. Once the rice is cooked, switch off the flame. At this stage, the rice may look slightly soft because the trapped steam is still carrying moisture.
7. Let it rest, covered, for 10 minutes. During this time, the steam redistributes evenly through the rice and the starch begins to set, making the grains light, fluffy and well separated.
8. Fluff gently with a fork and serve hot with Goan Pomfret Rava Fry.


Enjoy!
