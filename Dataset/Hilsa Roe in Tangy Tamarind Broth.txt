Hilsa Roe in Tangy Tamarind Broth
Ingredients
* Hilsa roe – 200–250 g
* Tamarind – about the size of a small lemon (soak in hot water for 10 minutes)
* Mustard oil – 2 tbsp
* Panch Phoron (Bengali five-spice blend) – ½ tsp
* Dried red chilies – 2
* Turmeric powder – ½ tsp
* Salt – to taste
* Sugar – 1½–2 tbsp (slightly more than the salt)
* Green chilies – for serving
Method
1. Mash the soaked tamarind with your fingers to extract the pulp. Do not strain it—the bits of pulp and skin add extra depth and tanginess to the dish.
2. Heat the mustard oil in a pan. Add the Panch Phoron and dried red chilies, allowing them to splutter and release their aroma.
3. Add the turmeric powder and immediately pour in the tamarind extract. Cover the pan with a lid.
4. Once it comes to a boil, uncover, add the salt and sugar, and let it simmer for 2 minutes.
5. Gently slide the raw hilsa roe into the broth. Cover and cook for 5 minutes. As it cooks, the roe will naturally firm up and hold its shape.
6. Remove from the heat and serve with fresh green chilies.
Tips
* If you plan to enjoy it the next day, make the broth slightly tangier, as the roe will gradually absorb the tamarind's sourness over time.
* This dish is at its best when mixed into hot steamed rice at the end of the meal, with a fresh green chili gently crushed into it for an extra burst of flavor.
