Goan Pomfret Rawa Fry


Ingredients:


* 2 whole pomfrets (cleaned)
* 8 Kashmiri dried red chillies
* 8 garlic cloves
* ½ inch ginger
* 1 tsp cumin seeds
* 1 tsp jaggery
* ½ tsp turmeric powder
* Salt to taste


For Coating:


* 1 tbsp rice flour
* 1 tbsp fine rava (semolina)
* Coconut oil for shallow frying


⸻


Method


1. Make 3–4 deep slits on both sides of the pomfret. Rub with salt, turmeric and lemon and keep aside for 10–15 minutes.
2. Dry roast the Kashmiri chillies, garlic, ginger, and cumin until aromatic, then blend them with jaggery, turmeric and salt into a smooth, thick paste.
3. Apply the masala generously all over the fish, inside the slits and the cavity. Marinate for at least 1 hour.
4. Mix rice flour and fine rava. Lightly coat the fish on both sides. The coating should be thin, not thick.
5. Heat coconut oil on a tawa over medium heat. Shallow fry the fish until golden and crisp on both sides. Avoid high heat so the masala caramelises without burning.
6. Fry a few curry leaves in the same oil and serve the pomfret hot with onion rings and coconut milk rice.


Enjoy!
